Apples

The apple is one of the pome (fleshy) fruits. Apples at harvest vary widely in size, shape, colour, and acidity, but most are fairly round and some shade of red or yellow. The thousands of varieties fall into three broad classes: cider, cooking, and dessert varieties.

Bananas

  • High in Potassium: Essential for maintaining heart and kidney health. Potassium helps balance fluids in the body, crucial for muscle function and nerve signals.
  • Energy Boost: With their natural sugars—glucose, fructose, and sucrose—they provide an immediate energy hit. Perfect for a pre-workout snack!
  • Heart Health: The combination of potassium and magnesium helps regulate blood pressure and support cardiovascular health.

    Watermelon

    Watermelon (Citrullus lanatus) is a flowering plant species of the Cucurbitaceae family and the name of its edible fruit. A scrambling and trailing vine-like plant, it is a highly cultivated fruit worldwide, with more than 1,000 varieties.
