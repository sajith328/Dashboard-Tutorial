Dhokla are fermented food

That’s a plate of traditional Gujarati snacks, featuring dhokla and khandvi. Dhokla are those soft, yellow, spongy cubes made from fermented rice and chickpea batter. Khandvi, the rolled yellow pieces topped with mustard seeds, is made from gram flour and yogurt. The plate also includes a couple of chutneys, fresh vegetables like carrots and cucumbers, and a sprig of cilantro. This mix is a vibrant representation of Gujarati cuisine.
